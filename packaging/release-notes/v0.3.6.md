Playlists, the library, and the queue take fewer extra clicks. Add a song without hunting for the destination, and open the artist you actually tapped.

## Changes

- Each credited artist in a track row and the player bar is its own link. A comma inside a name stays part of the name.
- Date added reads as Today, Yesterday, or N days ago for the first month, then as a calendar date.
- Settings can show the library as names-only rows and track tables as one-line rows without covers.
- The Add to playlist menu filters as you type. Adding a song that is already in a loaded playlist asks first.
- Drag the playing cover or title onto a playlist in the sidebar, or onto a gap in an open unsorted playlist, to add it there.
- The queue names the album, playlist, or Liked Songs the current song came from, with a link back.
- Home and Search cards have the same right-click actions as the rest of the app.
- The wheel over the volume slider moves it five percent at a time. Long device lists scroll.
- AUTO on the Winamp equalizer lays the bands flat. This still applies only to local Spotify playback.

Free-account alternate playback is unchanged.

## Downloads

Windows installers and portable ZIPs are available for x64 and ARM64. The macOS DMG contains a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel. Linux archives are available for x64 and ARM64. `checksums.txt` lists the SHA-256 of every download.

Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may warn about an unsigned download. See the [installation guide](https://master0ffate.github.io/oxidify/download/) for platform instructions.
