Top Songs no longer has to start with an empty page every time you reopen Oxidify. This update also adds a refresh button when you want fresh results from Spotify.

## Changes

- Top Songs can show up to 50 cached songs while Spotify refreshes them. The cache belongs to the verified account, expires after six hours, and stays under 1 MiB. This needs no extra registration or authorization.
- The visible Top Songs page gets priority over background discovery requests. A first launch without cached songs still depends on Spotify's response time and rate limits.
- A refresh icon sits just left of the Winamp mini player button. It reloads the current page, bypasses cached Top Songs and playlist rows, and repeats the current search when you are on Search. Older requests cannot overwrite the refreshed Top Songs cache. Refresh leaves downloaded audio, artwork, and sign-in credentials alone.
- Settings has a walkthrough for the optional personal Spotify app, including the exact redirect URI. Authorization now stays marked as pending until verification finishes.

## Downloads

Windows installers and portable ZIPs are available for x64 and ARM64. The macOS DMG contains a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel. Linux archives are available for x64 and ARM64. `checksums.txt` lists the SHA-256 of every download.

Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may warn about an unsigned download. See the [installation guide](https://master0ffate.github.io/oxidify/download/) for platform instructions.
