Track lists do less work while you browse, local Spotify playback gets two audio fixes, and updating Oxidify is easier to find.

## Changes

- Sorted and filtered track lists keep their prepared view between frames. The play button in Liked Songs now follows the displayed sort order.
- Reopening a playlist, removing songs, and loading another page of Top Songs invalidate the right cache entries, so old rows do not stick around.
- The equalizer compensates for overlapping bands. Its graph uses the same digital filters as local Spotify playback.
- Continuous sample-rate conversion targets packet-boundary crackle when the audio device runs at a different rate, such as 48 kHz on Windows.
- Press B to like or unlike the playing song. Typing and holding the key do not send repeated changes. Mouse side buttons navigate back and forward.
- The Update available button opens this repository's release page in your browser. Settings also has a Check for updates button. Checks ignore drafts and prereleases, time out after 15 seconds, and never install anything automatically.
- The Windows installer asks a running copy to quit cleanly before replacing it. Settings now uses the Oxidify mark.

Free-account alternate playback is unchanged. The EQ and sample-rate fixes above apply to local Spotify playback, not alternate audio.

## Downloads

Windows installers and portable ZIPs are available for x64 and ARM64. The macOS DMG contains a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel. Linux archives are available for x64 and ARM64. `checksums.txt` lists the SHA-256 of every download.

Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may warn about an unsigned download. See the [installation guide](https://master0ffate.github.io/oxidify/download/) for platform instructions.

These changes include backports from [Fastpotify](https://github.com/crmne/fastpotify), including Kveld.'s track-table caching work. Oxidify's alternate playback and account routing remain separate.
